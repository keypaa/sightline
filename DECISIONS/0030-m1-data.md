# DECISION-0030: M1 synthetic data balance and splits

Date: 2026-11-15
Status: accepted
Work package: M1a

## Context
M1 needs synthetic recognition data for color, shape and count, with balanced answers, held-out compositions and disjoint splits.

## Decision
- Tasks: color (8 classes), shape (4), count (1–5).
- 448x448 renders; a 4x4 corner tag makes every image unique (it does not touch the shape).
- Train/val/test template ids are disjoint (0 / 1 / 2).
- The held-out composition (red triangle) is in test, not train; the count 5 is in test, not train.
- The train/val answer counts are rebalanced after holding out the composition.
- The test has 512 color, 512 shape and 500 count items; train and val have 1,536 each.
- The manifest records the generator version, seed, per-example image sha256, task, question, answer, template id, split and properties.

## Evidence
- 6 tests pass: 500+ held-out per task, balanced answers, held-out compositions, matched pairs, manifest hash, no split overlap. [V]
- Generation: 1,536 train / 1,536 val / 1,524 test; 19.1 s, peak 3.6 GB, 19.6 CPU-s. [V]
- Manifest sha256: `a20be597caf75c042680545e524d108e36425cb54aaa19c75ce8bcb8d103f57b`. [V]

## Consequences
The M1b training can use the data; the G1 evaluation uses the held-out test.
